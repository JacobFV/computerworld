NSR3301  —  300 mA low-dropout linear regulator, 3.3 V fixed
Northstar-approved part, SOT-223-3 package         Rev 2.1, 2026-04

DESCRIPTION
  Fixed 3.3 V output, 300 mA, with fold-back current limit and thermal shutdown.
  Stable with a 1 uF or larger ceramic output capacitor.

ABSOLUTE MAXIMUM RATINGS
  Input voltage VIN ............................ -0.3 V to 16 V
  Output current ............................... internally limited
  Junction temperature ......................... 150 C
  Storage temperature .......................... -65 C to 150 C
  ESD (HBM) .................................... 2 kV

ELECTRICAL CHARACTERISTICS   (VIN = 5 V, CIN = 1 uF, COUT = 10 uF, TA = 25 C unless noted)
  Parameter                    Conditions                    Min     Typ     Max     Unit
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Output voltage               IOUT = 10 mA                  3.234   3.300   3.366   V
  Line regulation              VIN = 4 V to 12 V                     4       12      mV
  Load regulation              IOUT = 1 mA to 300 mA                 10      30      mV
  Dropout voltage              IOUT = 150 mA                         0.28    0.35    V
                               IOUT = 300 mA                         0.45    0.60    V
  Quiescent current            IOUT = 0                              2.1     3.5     mA
  Current limit                VOUT = 0 V                    350     480             mA
  Output noise                 10 Hz to 100 kHz                      40              uVrms
  PSRR                         f = 1 kHz                             62              dB
  Thermal shutdown                                                   160             C
  Thermal resistance R_thJA    SOT-223, 1 in^2 copper                52              K/W
                               SOT-223, minimal pad                  100             K/W

TYPICAL APPLICATION
  VIN --+-- CIN 1 uF --GND
        |
      [IN  NSR3301  OUT]--+-- COUT 10 uF -- GND
            |GND          |
            GND         3.3 V rail (add 100 nF at each load pin)

PIN ASSIGNMENT (SOT-223-3)
  1  IN
  2  GND  (tab)
  3  OUT

NOTES
  - Keep COUT within 5 mm of pin 3; ESR < 0.5 ohm.
  - Power dissipation P = (VIN - 3.3 V) * IOUT. At 5 V in and 250 mA out this is 0.43 W:
    allow ground copper under the tab (see R_thJA).
